## 1. Executive Summary

Following initial prompt-rescue evidence on 20 frames, we conducted a rigorous head-to-head comparison between detector-guided condition **A4** (RT-DETR-L largest cow box -> SAM 2.1) and condition **A5** (RT-DETR-L largest cow box + positive center point -> SAM 2.1) on **40 fresh, previously uninspected Kaggle Beef training samples** (10 per canonical behavior). Both A4 and A5 achieved an identical **92.5% mask-return rate (37/40)**; 100% of samples with a detected cow returned a non-empty mask. The sole 3 non-returns (7.5%) stemmed from upstream RT-DETR-L detection failures behind dense stanchion pipes (`beef_4_4_clip_0` in Drinking, `beef_131_5_clip_0` in Feeding, `beef_00000000580000000_27_clip_3` in Lying). Adding the center point prompt in A5 reduced mean connected components by **37.6% (19.6 vs 31.4)** while maintaining nearly identical mean mask coverage (0.2789 vs 0.2733). In accordance with protocol, **NO automated winner is selected**; the final qualitative verdict remains **PENDING HUMAN REVIEW** on the generated master contact sheet (`contact_sheet.jpg`).

## 2. Context & Motivation

In the baseline segmentation sanity check (`2026-09-23_behavior_primary_segmentation_sanity.md`), prompting SAM 2.1 with naive full crop boundaries (`[0, 0, 223, 223]`) failed critically on Kaggle Beef (50.0% zero-mask collapse, 118.3 mean connected components). The subsequent prompt-rescue audit (`2026-09-23_beef_sam_prompt_rescue.md`) on the original 20 frames proved that detector-guided bounding boxes (A4 and A5) restored plausible body localization and high coverage.

To verify whether A4 or A5 generalizes beyond the initial 20 samples, we expanded the evaluation to **40 fresh canonical training frames** (double the previous sample size) under strict experimental constraints:
1. Canonical TRAIN split only (`datasets/behavior/cvb_beef/train.csv`).
2. Absolute exclusion of all 20 previous Beef frames to prevent evaluation memorization or selection bias.
3. Balanced distribution: exactly 10 Drinking, 10 Feeding, 10 Lying, 10 Standing.
4. Maximization of unique recording sessions (`session_id`).
5. Identical 40 frames evaluated for both A4 and A5.
6. Zero test or validation set contamination.
7. No training or fine-tuning.

## 5. Forensic Analysis of Upstream RT-DETR-L Failures

Zero-shot RT-DETR-L (`rtdetr-l.pt`, COCO class 19 `cow`, confidence >= 0.25) failed to detect any cattle on exactly 3 of the 40 samples (7.5% failure rate):

1. **Sample #09 (`beef_4_4_clip_0` — Drinking, Session 4, frame 125):**
   - Head and upper torso deeply angled into water trough behind heavy metal stanchion dividers. Zero bounding boxes met the confidence threshold of 0.25.
   - Result: A4 and A5 recorded `mask_status = no_rtdetr_prompt` and `mask_returned = False`.
2. **Sample #13 (`beef_131_5_clip_0` — Feeding, Session 131, frame 125):**
   - Severe vertical bar obstruction across cow neck and muzzle during head-down feeding.
   - Result: A4 and A5 recorded `mask_status = no_rtdetr_prompt` and `mask_returned = False`.
3. **Sample #22 (`beef_00000000580000000_27_clip_3` — Lying, Session 580000000, frame 125):**
   - Sternal recumbency with dorsal surface fully occluded behind multiple intersecting tubular steel pen rails.
   - Result: A4 and A5 recorded `mask_status = no_rtdetr_prompt` and `mask_returned = False`.

**Key Observation:** In 100% of cases where RT-DETR-L produced a valid bounding box (37/37 samples), SAM 2.1 successfully returned a mask for both A4 and A5 with 0 downstream SAM crashes or zero-mask collapses.

## 6. Structural Differences: A4 vs A5

1. **Fragmentation & Noise Suppression:**
   - Under Condition A4 (box only), several challenging samples suffered severe pipe bleed and fragmentation. For example, in lying cow `beef_00000000111000000_2_clip_103`, A4 returned **177 connected components** (area ratio 0.27).
   - Under Condition A5 (box + center point), the positive foreground prompt anchored the mask onto the primary body mass, reducing connected components from **177 down to 67** (area ratio 0.51).
   - Overall, Condition A5 achieved a **37.6% reduction in mean connected components** (19.6 vs 31.4) across the 37 returned masks.
2. **Mask Consistency Across Behaviors:**
   - Standing cattle exhibited the lowest fragmentation under both conditions (A4 median 16.0, A5 median 16.0).
   - Lying and Feeding postures in dense pens continue to exhibit scattered mask fragments corresponding to stall bars, ear tags, and shavings litter.
3. **Qualitative Verification Required:**
   - Quantitative connected component count alone does not establish semantic superiority; human visual verification is required to determine whether A5's center point cuts off peripheral anatomy (limbs, snout, tail) or genuinely cleans up background clutter.
