# Phase 3 Pose Visual Review

Human visual-review sheet for inspecting the anatomical plausibility of predicted cattle keypoints from official DeepLabCut **SuperAnimal-Quadruped** across the three primary Phase 3 datasets:
* **ScienceDB** (Rear-view BCS)
* **MmCows** (CCTV behavior postures)
* **SideViewCows2026** (Parlor & barn Re-ID side views)

Two backbones evaluated on identical target-cow crops:
* **HRNet-W32** (`superanimal_quadruped_hrnet_w32.pt`)
* **ResNet-50** (`superanimal_quadruped_resnet_50.pt`)

> [!NOTE]
> This document is a human visual-review scorecard. No checkboxes are pre-filled, and no manual ratings are fabricated.
