\section{Interim Synthesis of Runs 1--6}

The completed single-task experiments show that cattle-centered inputs are useful, but not in an identical manner across the three tasks. For BCS, the matched automatic-perception configuration improved MAE and tolerance accuracy while leaving class-balanced measures slightly lower. For Behavior, foreground-guided temporal aggregation improved balanced accuracy, loss, and selected class/source metrics, but reduced overall accuracy and CVB Macro-F1. For Re-ID, oracle GT-mask guidance produced its largest improvement under the unconstrained Snapshot-to-Parlor condition and a smaller, mixed change under Barn-to-Parlor retrieval.

These findings support the scientific premise that the tasks require different information. They do not yet determine how much of that information should be shared in a unified model. The task-specific results therefore form the reference evidence for, rather than the conclusion of, the final MTL comparison.
