\section{Societal Impact}



The model should assist human decision making, not replace it. Poor identification matching can lead to observations being assigned to the wrong cow, and an inaccurate BCS or behavior forecast can cause to a delay in attention. A realistic system has to save the original image, the time, the confidence or proof of retrieval and a way for a person to judge the output. There still has to be good professional judgment in veterinary and welfare decisions.

Conditions at the farm will be an important aspect when using the system. Aspects like camera placement, accommodation, breeds, lighting, internet, and staff working procedure may vary from farm to farm. Due to this, the current findings cannot confirm the system's success in all areas including Bangladesh. Tests must be done by people in relative fields before claiming that the system works in real world.

Agricultural images may depict people, arrangements, or personal information. There is no guarantee in the license to utilize a database in research that the images may be distributed or used commercially. Privacy, ownership, and licensing regulations must be considered for each database and each potential field use.

\section{Ethical Issues}

Another important aspect in regard to ethical issues is distinguishing facts from hypotheses. The reason why ScienceDB is not cow-disjoint is that there is no cow ID that is scientifically proven in biology. In addition, the main Behavior analysis cannot be referred to as unseen cow analysis. Ground-truth masks are needed for Oracle segmentation-guided Re-ID experiments; therefore, it cannot be used as an example of automatic segmentation. Successes, failures, and partially successful cases are considered in this research instead of only positive ones.

In each case of data reuse, individual evaluation should be carried out. Data set registry provides information on the existing licensing conditions and access, however, all conditional permissions should be evaluated before redistribution or utilization. This thesis does not imply ethics approval and user field consent.

The validation data is used for the selection of the model, while the held-out test data is used only for evaluation. The external test data should never be used again for tuning after its performance was estimated. It is important to note if some data instances were not included because of the perceptual failure. The authors of the paper are the final proprietors of their work even though they utilize the AI technology to write the texts or program. However, the authors have to ensure that their project is correct, true, and corresponds to the university policies regarding the use of AI technology and the citation rules.

There is a list of the main scientific and operational risks in Table~\ref{tab:risks}. The evaluation design also includes some task-level controls that are aimed at avoiding the mentioned problems. ScienceDB burst groups and Behavior videos/sessions remain in the corresponding data partitions. The identities of the biological cows in the SideView Protocol A that are used for representation learning are separated from the identities of evaluation. Matched samples are used in comparing RGB, perception-based BCS, and Behavior models. Re-ID is an oracle guided model when ground truth masks are used. Some limitations of the experiments have been identified. ScienceDB lacks confirmed cow identities, Walking has only one source, and each task model is based on a single run. In the final MTL comparison, the task evaluation protocols should remain comparable, and any changes in the training budget should also be stated.
