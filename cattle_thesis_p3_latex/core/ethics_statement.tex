\section*{Ethics Statement}
This study uses existing cattle image and video datasets. It concerns dataset auditing, model development and benchmark evaluation. It does not establish clinical effectiveness or authorize automated treatment decisions. Dataset permissions and restrictions must be respected independently of whether a download is technically possible.
\par\medskip
The report distinguishes released biological identities from temporary tracking identifiers and completed experiments from proposed work. Results and limitations are linked to project records in Appendix~\ref{app:evidence}. These practices are intended to prevent unsupported claims about animal identity, generalization and model reliability.
