\section*{Ethics Statement}

This research is one of the requirements of the B.Sc. degree in Computer Science and Engineering. The sources of all data used in the research, as well as benchmarks used in computations, have been properly cited in the bibliography.

The research was based only on public data available on the internet that contains pictures and video of cows. The data includes ScienceDB, CVB, Kaggle Beef, and SideViewCows2026 datasets. Invasive experiments on live animals have not been conducted. The aim of the research is to validate automated vision techniques and multitask deep learning models. The outcome of the experiment cannot be regarded as evidence of clinical efficiency or justification of autonomous therapy.

The individuals and institutions that contributed to this research in terms of support and assistance have received proper recognition. The research followed the university's ethical code of conduct and posed no ethical issues.
