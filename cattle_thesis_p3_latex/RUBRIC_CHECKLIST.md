## Visible evidence of rubric alignment

- Chapter 1 contains background, motivation, Phase 2 limitations, the research problem, objectives, questions, methodology in brief, scope, challenges, and supported pre-MTL contributions.
- Chapter 2 synthesizes related work by theme rather than as an article-by-article list and ends with explicit research gaps.
- Chapter 3 separates functional, non-functional, and scientific requirements and discusses societal, environmental, ethical, management, risk, and economic constraints without inventing stakeholder evidence.
- Chapter 4 documents data roles, harmonization, split protection, perception selection, all six executed task models, metrics, reproducibility, compute policy, and the planned MTL boundary.
- Chapter 5 reports full and matched BCS/Behavior controls, source/class trade-offs, separate Barn/Snapshot Re-ID tables, coverage, negative findings, design adjustments, statistical limits, and task-specific discussion.
- Chapter 6 remains explicitly provisional because MTL-dependent research questions and conclusions cannot yet be answered.
