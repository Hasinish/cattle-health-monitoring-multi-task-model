## Visible evidence of rubric alignment

- Chapter 1 contains background, motivation, research problem, calibrated objectives, exact research questions, methodology in brief, scope, challenges, and integrated contributions.
- Chapter 2 synthesizes related work by theme rather than as an article-by-article list and ends with explicit research gaps.
- Chapter 3 separates functional, non-functional, and scientific requirements and discusses societal, environmental, ethical, management, risk, and economic constraints without inventing stakeholder evidence.
- Chapter 4 documents data roles, split protection, perception selection, single-task models, and all executed multi-task configurations (E1 hard-shared, E3 modular task-private, E4 PCGrad optimization control) with parameter allocations and training protocols.
- Chapter 5 reports full and matched single-task controls, source/class trade-offs, separate Barn/Snapshot Re-ID tables, coverage, negative findings, complete E1/E3/E4 multi-task results, direct PCGrad gradient diagnostics, design decisions, statistical boundaries, and negative-transfer synthesis.
- Chapter 6 provides final answers to all three research questions, six distinct contributions, twelve methodological limitations, seven future work directions, and final thesis conclusion.

The rubric’s historical chapter-number typos are interpreted by section title while preserving the supplied six-chapter structure. CO13 depends on actual teamwork evidence; CO8 and CO14 include presentation/demo obligations that prose alone cannot satisfy.
